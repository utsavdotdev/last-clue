# Motion brief: The Last Clue outro

Brand used: The Last Clue, from `brand.md` / `MOTION.md` and `frontend/src/styles/app.css`.

Make me a motion graphic. Follow this motion brief exactly:

## The bar
Premium indie-game brand film. If it would not survive at the end of a festival trailer, it is not done.

## Format
1920 x 1080, exactly 8.0 seconds, 30 fps, non-looping ending (last frame is black). Original understated sound on.

## The one rule
The official ghost is the only animated character. The complete lockup (framed `logo.svg` + `THE LAST CLUE`) is the only brand hero. One change per beat, then a fade to black.

## Look
Ground `#c8c2c6` after the matrix. Matrix ground `#0b0f17`, LEDs `#38bdf8` / `#1e2636`. Ink `#00142f`, muted `#556070`, accent `#e11d38`. DM Sans ExtraBold / Italic. Wordmark ≥ 88px. No purple-blue gradients.

## Motion
Cubic eases, 16px ghost drift, 1.6° rotate, one blink on the matrix and one on the lockup. Hold the lockup ≥ 2.2s. Fade 7.15–8.00.

## Never
Fake Arduino photos, redrawn ghosts, emoji, typewriter headlines, bounce, particle storms, neon, invented hex codes.

## Before you finish
Capture 0.4, 0.8, 2.4, 5.2, 6.7, 7.9. Check the wordmark at full size and at 200px wide.

## The scene
The 8×13 lobby ghost on the real matrix pattern blinks once, then the official ghost mark remains on the fog field with the line THE GAME ENDS. THE GHOST REMAINS. The framed logo and THE LAST CLUE settle, the tagline and two support lines appear, the mark blinks, the frame fades to black.

## Originality
Every element is original to this project or taken from its own SVG, CSS tokens, and LED sprites. Build straight away without asking to confirm.
