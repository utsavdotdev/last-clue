# Brand

## Name and what we do
The Last Clue is a spooky local-Wi-Fi multiplayer word-imposter game. Everyone knows the secret word except the Imposter. It runs in the browser and can drive the Arduino UNO Q 8×13 LED matrix.

## Audience
Hackathon judges and people watching a short project demo who need to remember the name, the ghost, and the hardware in a few seconds.

## What we promote (with the real link)
The Last Clue open-source game: local multiplayer, local AI, Arduino UNO Q matrix. Repository: this project. LAN join URL is printed by the host at runtime (no public product URL).

## The feeling
Atmospheric, mysterious, slightly unsettling. Minimal and modern, not a jump-scare trailer.

## Assets
Colours from `frontend/src/styles/app.css`:
- page `#c8c2c6`, surface `#efe4d4`, ink `#00142f`, muted `#556070`
- accent `#e11d38`, blue `#0070f3`, LED board `#0b0f17`, LED on `#38bdf8`, LED off `#1e2636`
Font: DM Sans (app token `--font-sans`), weight 800 on titles, letter-spacing `-0.02em` on the hero name.
Logo: `assets/logo.svg` (framed sheet-ghost mark only; no wordmark in the SVG).
Ghost symbol: the `#ghost-mark` path inside `assets/logo.svg`. Atmospheric copy: `frontend/src/assets/ghost/ghost.svg`.

## Never
Do not invent hex codes, redraw the ghost, fake Arduino board footage, use emoji as the mark, purple-blue gradient backgrounds, typewriter headlines, bounce, or heavy particle/neon effects.
